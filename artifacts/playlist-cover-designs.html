Fan Stack — Albums spread in a bottom-left to top-right fan. The frontmost album is the dominant/primary cover. Cards behind it peek out with decreasing opacity. Feels like physical vinyl records spread on a table. Works well for 2–4 images; gracefully degrades to a single card.

✓ PROS

  • Visually rich, storytelling feel
  • Depth without harsh cuts
  • Great on grid cards

✗ CONS

  • Bottom art partially hidden
  • Small at 52dp row thumbnail

Rotated Stack — All album arts stacked at the same position, with the back images rotated ±4–8°. The top card is perfectly straight with a prominent shadow. Spotify uses a subtle version of this. Clean, modern, minimal.

✓ PROS

  • Clean centre composition
  • Elegant, Spotify-familiar
  • Works at any size

✗ CONS

  • Only top art fully visible
  • Others mainly decorative

Diagonal Split — Images divided by angled clip-paths. No hard vertical/horizontal cuts; a subtle rotation (10–15°) on the dividing line makes it feel dynamic rather than mechanical. This fixes the original "slash looks bad" problem — the angle is intentional design, not an accident.

✓ PROS

  • Fixes the harsh vertical cut
  • Dynamic, editorial feel
  • Each album art fully seen

✗ CONS

  • Edge art slightly cropped
  • Darker seam line needed

Mosaic with Gap — The classic 2×2 grid but with a 3px gap between tiles. The gap is the background colour (#15161D) so it acts as a border/frame between images. The layouts adapt: 2 images go top/bottom (horizontal split), 3 images get a wide top tile + 2 bottom tiles, 4 images get equal 2×2. This is the OpenTune approach but with the breathing gap that makes it feel intentional rather than crammed.

✓ PROS

  • Each art visible equally
  • Gap makes it feel designed
  • Familiar (Spotify/Apple)
  • Works perfectly at 52dp

✗ CONS

  • Requires exactly 4 arts for the "full" look

Blur Stage — The dominant album art is blurred full-bleed as a background. The individual album arts are then layered as floating thumbnails on top (the primary one slightly larger in the centre). Creates an ambient, immersive feel — like Apple Music's player background. Rare to see on playlist list items, stands out.

✓ PROS

  • Most visually rich option
  • Feels premium / editorial
  • No harsh cuts at all

✗ CONS

  • Heavy in a long list
  • Small thumbnails hard to see at 52dp
  • Most complex to implement

Circle Cluster — Album arts rendered as overlapping circles of different sizes, arranged in a natural cluster (large front-left, medium top-right, small bottom). The overlapping border (same as background colour) creates separation. Unique, playful, and avoids any cuts whatsoever.

✓ PROS

  • Completely avoids cuts
  • Playful, distinctive look
  • Arts clearly identifiable

✗ CONS

  • Not rectangular — wastes corner space
  • Feels less "music app" standard
